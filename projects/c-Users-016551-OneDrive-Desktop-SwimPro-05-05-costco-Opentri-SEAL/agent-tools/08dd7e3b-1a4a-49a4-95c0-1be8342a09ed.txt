Costco Statistics 2026: Shopper Demographics & Revenue

Costco is one of the largest retailers in the world, with 132 million active customers across 14 countries.

Costco, earlier called the "Price Club, was started in 1976. It went on to become the first company to reach $3 billion in sales in less than six years since its launch. Today, the retail brand has an annual revenue of $269.9 billion and has 914 stores/warehouses across the globe. Costco statistics reveal that the company employs over 341,000 people and is the 5th largest retailer worldwide.

Let us take a look at Costco in numbers.

## Top Costco Statistics In 2025

- Costco operates 914 warehouses globally and is the 3rd largest retailer in the US as of 2025.
- Costco has 145 million members worldwide in 2025.
- Costco has registered a revenue of $269.9 billion as of September 2025.
- Salt Lake City, Utah, has the largest Costco store in the world, spread over 235,000 square feet.
- 46% of the total Costco shopper base belongs to the middle-income category.
- Costco’s e-commerce sales grew by 20.7% compared to last year.
- There was a growth of 32% in Costco app downloads and a 16% increase in website traffic as of Q2 2025.
